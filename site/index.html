Assay

Cross-checks Korean listed company filings against independent audit reports, OpenDART XBRL, credit ratings and public records.

Paste a financial statement copied from a filing, in Korean or English. Assay reads it and runs its arithmetic and scoring checks. Everything happens in this page, and its security policy blocks anything from being sent out.

Reports generated by the command line tool from public filings. A flag marks something to read more closely. It does not mean anyone did anything wrong.

Empirical evaluation across 39 company-years (31 Korean listed companies).
